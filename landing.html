THE NARRATIVE

THE GRAND HEIST

HOW THE CAT LORDS TOOK EVERYTHING

In the ancient golden age of DogeOS, prosperity flowed unchecked. But while the Shibes slept, the ruthless Fat Cat Syndicate led by the dread Cat Lord orchestrated the ultimate theft: they seized the planetary vaults, locked the Doge community treasury behind reinforced obsidian gates, and posted demonic legionnaires across the ruins.

"They locked the vault. They thought we would roll over. Now we raid the dungeon, tear down the gate, and take back every last Doge."
— Elder Shibe, Keeper of the Heist

You are an insurgent Shibe operating from a hidden camp. You must gather wood, mine raw iron, forge weaponry, swarm the dungeon gates in co-op strike teams, slay the Cat Lord, and extract with genuine on-chain treasures before the chamber collapses.

TOKENOMICS

THE PURE ON-CHAIN ECONOMY

Two kinds of money, kept apart: in-game DOGE coins from quests and drops buy camp upgrades, and real Testnet DOGE in your wallet pays for every on-chain action.

REAL TESTNET DOGE

Minting, pool trades, staking and your class oath all cost real testnet DOGE. Your wallet balance is read straight from the DogeOS Chikyū chain, and every transaction is signed in your own wallet.

80 / 20 FEE SPLIT

Each 1 DOGE mint fee is split by the contract: 80% to the Staking Pool, 20% to the treasury. Pool trades pay 3%: 2% to liquidity providers and 1% protocol fee, split the same 80/20.

REDSTONE ORACLE

Live DOGE/USD price from the RedStone feed (`0x434F…C968`). When your wallet is connected it anchors the in-game market's mood; pool swap prices always come from the pool reserves.

WEB3 GEAR & NFTS

DOGEMASTER MEME FORGE & REROLL

Defeat the dungeon raid to unlock the legendary DogeMaster NPC. Mint verified ERC-721 gear NFTs for 1 Testnet DOGE, imbued with deterministic meme stats or burn them to reroll!

MEME STAT GENERATION

Every gear NFT's bonus is derived from its token ID, so anyone can check it. Rarity (common ×1 up to legendary ×6) scales the roll. Meme rolls include:

BLAZE 420: +420 HP NICE 69: +69 ATK LUCKY 777: +77 HP MUCH 4.2: +0.42 DEF SPEED 6.9: +6.9 ATK

Cursed rolls exist too: HANGOVER 420 (−42 HP), CURSED 69 (−6.9 ATK), PAPER 4.2 (−0.42 DEF).

BURN-TO-REROLL ENGINE

Rolled a curse like HANGOVER 420? Feed it to the DogeMaster's furnace. The Burn-to-Reroll mechanism burns your debuffed ERC-721 NFT on-chain and triggers a fresh random mint.

Web3 Custody Permanence: Your gear NFTs live safely in your crypto wallet. Unlike raw materials, NFTs NEVER drop on PvP death.

RESOURCE ECONOMY

RAW GATHERING & FISHING

Gather raw resources in contested monster territories. Nodes are finite and renewable on strict cooldown timers.

STEEL / IRON

Every rock you gather also gives 1 iron, and monsters sometimes drop it. Essential for crafting weapons and armor.

WOOD

Face a tree, press F and stand still for 3.5 seconds to gather. Each tree needs 14 seconds to regrow, and taking a hit cancels the gather.

STONE

Excavated from boulders scattered across the plains. Used in hammer, armor and charm recipes at the forge.

RAW FISH

Face the water, press F and stand still to fish. Restores HP and supplies sustenance for prolonged dungeon expeditions.

BLOOD IN THE WATER

FULL-LOOT PVP RISK

DEATH MEANS LOSS: PROTECT YOUR LOOT

In multiplayer, the wilds and the ruins are PvP zones: outside camp, players who are not in your party can attack you. Rival players can ambuscade your squad while you battle bosses or mine iron.

  • Loot Drops on Death: If you are slain by another player or monster, up to 3 of each carried material (Steel, Wood, Stone, Whiskers) drop as a pile anyone can loot. The rest is lost.
  • Safe Camp Haven: The the camp behind the wooden spiked perimeter fence is 100% safe. Enemies and enemy projectiles cannot cross the barrier. Hold [R] to rest: HP back to 60%, MP to full.
  • NFT Invulnerability: On-chain ERC-721 gear NFTs stay bound to your crypto wallet address and will NEVER drop on death.
MULTIPLAYER RAID CYCLE

8-PLAYER SERVER RAID & LOCKOUT

To ensure peak game balance and low latency, each DOGEON server instance is strictly capped at 8 simultaneous players.

30-MINUTE EXTRACTION WINDOW

From the moment you start playing, a 30-minute raid timer counts down. Players must assemble, coordinate professions, battle through Cat Minions, breach the inner sanctum, and slay the Cat Lord before time expires.

5-HOUR GATE SEAL LOCKOUT

If the 30-minute timer runs out before the Cat Lord falls, the raid FAILS! Any players inside the chamber are violently ejected back to the ruins, and the gates seal shut for 5 hours. Players can still gather, fish, level skills, and trade in the overworld while waiting for the next raid window.
