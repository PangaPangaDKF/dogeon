01 // THE NARRATIVE

THE GRAND HEIST

HOW THE CAT LORDS TOOK EVERYTHING

In the ancient golden age of DogeOS, prosperity flowed unchecked. But while the Shibes slept, the ruthless Fat Cat Syndicate led by the dread Cat Lord orchestrated the ultimate theft: they seized the planetary vaults, locked the Doge community treasury behind reinforced obsidian gates, and posted demonic legionnaires across the ruins.

"They locked the vault. They thought we would roll over. Now we raid the dungeon, tear down the gate, and take back every last Doge."
— Elder Shibe, Keeper of the Heist

You are an insurgent Shibe operating from the clandestine Camp Refuge. You must chop timber, mine raw iron, forge weaponry, swarm the dungeon gates in co-op strike teams, slay the Cat Lord, and extract with genuine on-chain treasures before the chamber collapses.

07 // BLOOD IN THE WATER

FULL-LOOT PVP RISK

DEATH MEANS LOSS: PROTECT YOUR LOOT

The Wilderness and Dungeon Ruins are non-consensual PvP combat zones. Rival players can ambuscade your squad while you battle bosses or mine iron.

  • Loot Drops on Death: If you are slain by another player or monster, all carried raw materials (Steel, Wood, Stone, Whiskers) drop onto the ground as lootable pickups for anyone to plunder!
  • Safe Camp Haven: The Camp Refuge behind the wooden spiked perimeter fence is 100% safe. Enemies and enemy projectiles cannot cross the barrier. Press [R] to rest and recover HP/MP.
  • NFT Invulnerability: On-chain ERC-721 gear NFTs stay bound to your crypto wallet address and will NEVER drop on death.
08 // MULTIPLAYER RAID CYCLE

8-PLAYER SERVER RAID & LOCKOUT

To ensure peak game balance and low latency, each DOGEON server instance is strictly capped at 8 simultaneous players.

⏱️

30-MINUTE EXTRACTION WINDOW

When the dungeon gates grind open, an active 30-minute raid timer starts ticking. Players must assemble, coordinate professions, battle through Cat Minions, breach the inner sanctum, and slay the Cat Lord before time expires.

🔒

5-HOUR GATE SEAL LOCKOUT

If the 30-minute timer runs out before the Cat Lord falls, the raid FAILS! Any players inside the chamber are violently ejected back to the ruins, and the gates seal shut for 5 hours. Players can still gather, fish, level skills, and trade in the overworld while waiting for the next raid window.
